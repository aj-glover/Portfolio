Challenge

Client: National Alliance of Wound Care and Ostomy (NAWCO) — a nonprofit organization providing wound care certification to healthcare professionals.

Problem: NAWCO needed to grow digital awareness and drive certification applications through online channels. The existing website lacked SEO optimization, paid media strategy, and conversion tracking.

Success Criteria: Increase qualified traffic, boost certification applications, and establish measurable ROI from digital marketing spend.

Research & Discovery

Audience
Registered nurses, nurse practitioners, and healthcare administrators seeking accredited wound care credentials.
Competitor Analysis
Evaluated competing certification bodies, their search visibility, and content gaps in the wound care education space.
Customer Journey
Mapped the path from "wound care certification" search query → eligibility check → application → payment → certification.
Keyword Research
Identified high-intent keywords: "wound care certification," "NAWCO," "WCC certification," "accredited wound care certification programs."

Strategy

1SEO Optimization
Targeted high-volume keywords; optimized landing pages for search visibility.
↓
2Content Strategy
Built certification requirement pages, eligibility checker tools, and educational content.
↓
3Landing Pages
/wound-care-certification (1.2M impressions), /nutrition-wound-care-certification (523K impressions), and eligibility checker (440K impressions).
↓
4Paid Search
Google Ads campaigns targeting wound care certification keywords; 974K impressions, 86.4K clicks.
↓
5Analytics & Optimization
Continuous monitoring of conversion rates, traffic sources, and user behavior to refine strategy.

Video Overview

Execution

Google Ads Campaigns: Top campaign — National Alliance of WoundCare (90K sessions). Secondary campaign — NAWCCB Search 2024 (3.3K sessions).

Landing Page Optimization: Top pages by impressions: /wound-care-certification (1.2M), /nutrition-wound-care-certification (523K), /examination-eligibility-checker (440K).

Conversion Funnel: Check WCC Eligibility (14,747) → Apply (10,592) → Check Recertification (6,792) → Payment Portal (516).

Traffic Sources: Google (179K sessions), Bing (29K), woundcareuniversity.com referral (5.2K), wcei.net referral (4.8K), Yahoo (4.2K).

Lessons Learned

What Worked
Organic search was the #1 channel (189K sessions), validating the SEO-first approach. Google dominated search traffic (179K sessions). Display ads proved effective for top-of-funnel awareness (62K sessions).
What Could Improve
Email marketing was underutilized (only 232 new users). Social media showed minimal contribution (1.6K organic social). Paid search (23K new users) could scale further with higher budget allocation.
Recommendations
Expand email nurture sequences for exam-eligible candidates. Test video content for organic social. Scale Google Ads budget against proven high-ROI campaigns.
